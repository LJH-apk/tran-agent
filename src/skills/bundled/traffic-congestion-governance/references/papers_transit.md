# 公交优先与多模式走廊治理：核心论文证据库

> 本文件仅提供题录、摘要级重述和治理解读，不复制论文正文。需要精确算法、参数、实验数值时必须回到原文核验。

## 阅读规则

- 先看“治理启示”和“误用警告”，再决定是否需要读原文。
- 顶刊/高引不等于在你的城市、路网和需求下必然有效。
- 实地研究 > 校准良好的仿真 > 单一理想化算例，证据等级必须在推荐中体现。

## T01 · Bus Lanes with Intermittent Priority: Strategy Formulae and an Evaluation

- **作者 / 年份**：M. Eichler, C. F. Daganzo (2006)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part B: Methodological
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.trb.2005.10.001
- **影响力定位**：Influential analytical bus-priority paper.
- **研究问题**：How to prioritize buses without wasting road capacity when bus frequency is moderate.
- **治理启示**：Evaluate person-delay, not vehicle-delay; intermittent priority can outperform rigid lane dedication in specific demand ranges.
- **适用场景**：Use for bus lanes, intermittent lanes, and arterial priority design.
- **误用警告**：Not suitable near or beyond severe saturation; car-lane capacity loss can dominate.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trb.2005.10.001

## T02 · Multi-Modal Traffic Signal Control with Priority, Signal Actuation and Coordination

- **作者 / 年份**：Q. He, K. L. Head, J. Ding (2014)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.trc.2014.05.001
- **影响力定位**：Highly cited multimodal signal-control paper.
- **研究问题**：Jointly serving buses, cars, and pedestrians in coordinated signal systems.
- **治理启示**：Priority should be conditional and network-aware; combine actuation, coordination, and multimodal priority rather than treating TSP as an isolated exception.
- **适用场景**：Use for corridors with buses and pedestrian constraints.
- **误用警告**：Priority can transfer delay to side streets or pedestrians if person-based objectives are omitted.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2014.05.001

## T03 · Analytical Formulation and Empirical Evaluation of Pre-Signals for Bus Priority

- **作者 / 年份**：S. I. Guler, M. Menendez (2014)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part B: Methodological
- **DOI / 来源**：https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/transb/v64y2014icp41-53.html
- **影响力定位**：High-quality TR-B analytical/empirical bus-priority paper.
- **研究问题**：Bus priority when a dedicated lane wastes capacity.
- **治理启示**：Upstream pre-signals can dynamically reorganize lane use and reduce person delay under suitable conditions.
- **适用场景**：Use for constrained urban arterials and bus approaches to signals.
- **误用警告**：Geometric space, compliance, queue storage, and pedestrian interactions must be checked.
- **原始链接**：https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/transb/v64y2014icp41-53.html

## T04 · Bus Priority at Signalized Intersections with Single-Lane Approaches: A Novel Pre-Signal Strategy

- **作者 / 年份**：S. I. Guler, V. V. Gayah, M. Menendez (2016)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies
- **DOI / 来源**：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0968090X15004210
- **影响力定位**：Representative top-journal bus-priority design paper.
- **研究问题**：Providing priority when lane allocation is severely constrained.
- **治理启示**：Evaluate operating domains where person-delay improves; priority is not universally beneficial under all saturation levels.
- **适用场景**：Use for single-lane approaches and dynamic lane allocation concepts.
- **误用警告**：Check oversaturation and queue-blocking thresholds explicitly.
- **原始链接**：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0968090X15004210

## T05 · Transit Signal Priority Control at Signalized Intersections: A Comprehensive Review

- **作者 / 年份**：Y. Lin, X. Yang, N. Zou, M. Franz (2014)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Letters
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1179/1942787514Y.0000000044
- **影响力定位**：Useful comprehensive TSP review with field evidence.
- **研究问题**：Taxonomy, evaluation methods, and evidence for transit signal priority.
- **治理启示**：Separate passive, active rule-based, and model-based priority; evaluate with analytical, simulation, and field evidence.
- **适用场景**：Use for TSP strategy selection and literature grounding.
- **误用警告**：Field benefits vary with saturation, bus frequency, schedule adherence, and coordination.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1179/1942787514Y.0000000044

## T06 · Coordinated Transit Signal Priority Supporting Transit Progression under Connected Vehicle Technology

- **作者 / 年份**：J. Hu, B. B. Park, Y.-J. Lee (2015)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.trc.2014.12.005
- **影响力定位**：Influential connected-vehicle TSP / corridor coordination paper.
- **研究问题**：How to preserve bus priority benefits across consecutive signals.
- **治理启示**：Transit priority should be corridor-coordinated and preferably person-delay-aware rather than granting isolated priority at each junction.
- **适用场景**：Use for connected-bus priority and multimodal corridor control.
- **误用警告**：Benefits decrease under heavy saturation; protect general-traffic storage and downstream capacity.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2014.12.005

## T07 · Development and Evaluation of a Coordinated and Conditional Bus Priority Approach

- **作者 / 年份**：W. Ma, X. Yang, Y. Liu (2010)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Record
- **DOI / 来源**：10.3141/2145-06
- **影响力定位**：Field-evaluated coordinated conditional bus-priority approach.
- **研究问题**：How to coordinate priority for early/late buses across intersection groups.
- **治理启示**：Conditional priority can improve reliability with smaller side effects than unconditional priority; schedule/headway state should influence grants.
- **适用场景**：Use for bus-reliability governance and priority logic design.
- **误用警告**：Transfer requires accurate bus prediction and local signal constraints.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.3141/2145-06

## T08 · Transit Signal Priority with Connected Vehicle Technology

- **作者 / 年份**：J. Hu, B. B. Park, A. E. Parkany (2014)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Record
- **DOI / 来源**：10.3141/2418-03
- **影响力定位**：Connected-vehicle TSP application paper.
- **研究问题**：How two-way bus-signal communication and passenger data can improve TSP.
- **治理启示**：Reallocate rather than simply add green, and consider person delay / passenger count when prioritizing transit.
- **适用场景**：Use for multimodal signal KPIs and connected transit priority.
- **误用警告**：Connected-vehicle penetration and communication assumptions must be explicit.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.3141/2418-03
