# 需求管理、拥堵定价与停车治理：核心论文证据库

> 本文件仅提供题录、摘要级重述和治理解读，不复制论文正文。需要精确算法、参数、实验数值时必须回到原文核验。

## 阅读规则

- 先看“治理启示”和“误用警告”，再决定是否需要读原文。
- 顶刊/高引不等于在你的城市、路网和需求下必然有效。
- 实地研究 > 校准良好的仿真 > 单一理想化算例，证据等级必须在推荐中体现。

## D01 · The Fundamental Law of Road Congestion: Evidence from US Cities

- **作者 / 年份**：G. Duranton, M. A. Turner (2011)
- **期刊 / 会议**：American Economic Review
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1257/aer.101.6.2616
- **影响力定位**：Highly cited AER empirical paper on induced traffic.
- **研究问题**：Whether road expansion reduces congestion in the long run.
- **治理启示**：Added lane-km can induce roughly proportional additional vehicle travel in the studied US context; do not evaluate expansion with fixed-demand assumptions only.
- **适用场景**：Use for capacity-expansion appraisal and induced-demand checks.
- **误用警告**：Empirical magnitude is context-specific; do not mechanically transfer elasticity to every city or corridor.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.101.6.2616

## D02 · A Review of the Evidence for Induced Travel and Changes in Transportation and Environmental Policy in the US and the UK

- **作者 / 年份**：R. B. Noland, L. L. Lem (2002)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part D: Transport and Environment
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/S1361-9209(01)00009-8
- **影响力定位**：Widely cited review of induced travel.
- **研究问题**：Evidence and mechanisms behind travel induced by new capacity.
- **治理启示**：Capacity changes can trigger short-run behavioral and long-run land-use responses; forecasts should include elastic demand and scenario ranges.
- **适用场景**：Use for long-horizon road investment and demand forecasting.
- **误用警告**：Induced travel is not identical to induced congestion; welfare and accessibility can still change positively or negatively.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/S1361-9209(01)00009-8

## D03 · Disappearing Traffic? The Story So Far

- **作者 / 年份**：S. Cairns, S. Atkins, P. Goodwin (2002)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Proceedings of the ICE - Municipal Engineer
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1680/muen.2002.151.1.13
- **影响力定位**：Influential evidence synthesis on road-space reallocation.
- **研究问题**：What happens when road space is removed or reassigned.
- **治理启示**：Traffic may redistribute, retime, remode, or disappear rather than simply displacing one-for-one; evaluate behavioral adaptation instead of assuming fixed trips.
- **适用场景**：Use for pedestrianization, bus lanes, cycle lanes, road diets, and access restrictions.
- **误用警告**：Outcomes vary substantially by alternatives, network redundancy, enforcement, and complementary measures.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1680/muen.2002.151.1.13

## D04 · Cruising for Parking

- **作者 / 年份**：D. C. Shoup (2006)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transport Policy
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.tranpol.2006.05.005
- **影响力定位**：Highly cited parking-policy paper.
- **研究问题**：Traffic created by drivers searching for underpriced curb parking.
- **治理启示**：Parking management is congestion management; curb pricing and occupancy targets can remove search traffic and reduce local circulation.
- **适用场景**：Use for CBD congestion, curb management, school/hospital districts, and commercial streets.
- **误用警告**：Pricing requires loading-zone design, enforcement, equity treatment, and off-street substitution analysis.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tranpol.2006.05.005

## D05 · Traffic Restraint, Road Pricing and Network Equilibrium

- **作者 / 年份**：H. Yang, M. G. H. Bell (1997)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part B: Methodological
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/S0191-2615(96)00030-6
- **影响力定位**：Classic TR-B network-pricing paper.
- **研究问题**：How tolls can restrain demand while respecting network equilibrium.
- **治理启示**：Toll design should be solved with network equilibrium and elastic demand; many toll patterns can meet a target, so secondary objectives matter.
- **适用场景**：Use for cordon, link, and area pricing design.
- **误用警告**：Revenue, distribution, evasion, and political feasibility are outside pure equilibrium optimality.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/S0191-2615(96)00030-6

## D06 · Traffic Congestion Pricing Methodologies and Technologies

- **作者 / 年份**：A. de Palma, R. Lindsey (2011)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.trc.2011.02.010
- **影响力定位**：Highly cited TR-C overview/review.
- **研究问题**：Design choices and technology options for congestion pricing.
- **治理启示**：Pricing architecture must jointly specify spatial scope, time differentiation, vehicle classes, detection, billing, and enforcement.
- **适用场景**：Use for practical pricing-system design and technology selection.
- **误用警告**：Operational feasibility and public acceptance can dominate theoretical first-best designs.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2011.02.010

## D07 · Reducing Urban Road Transportation Externalities: Road Pricing in Theory and in Practice

- **作者 / 年份**：A. Anas, R. Lindsey (2011)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Review of Environmental Economics and Policy
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1093/reep/req019
- **影响力定位**：Influential policy review connecting theory and implementation.
- **研究问题**：Using pricing to internalize congestion, pollution, accidents, and other externalities.
- **治理启示**：Pricing can deliver efficiency gains, but distribution and acceptance are first-class design constraints; revenue use is part of the policy.
- **适用场景**：Use for policy packages and equity-aware governance.
- **误用警告**：Do not evaluate pricing by travel-time savings alone.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1093/reep/req019

## D08 · Area-based Equitable Pricing Strategies for Multimodal Urban Networks with Heterogeneous Users

- **作者 / 年份**：M. Liu et al. (2020)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part A: Policy and Practice
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.tra.2020.04.009
- **影响力定位**：Representative modern equity-aware congestion-pricing paper.
- **研究问题**：Balancing congestion efficiency and distributional equity among users with different values of time.
- **治理启示**：Equity can be placed directly in the optimization objective rather than checked only after pricing is designed.
- **适用场景**：Use when proposing area pricing or policy packages to decision makers.
- **误用警告**：Equity metric choice is normative; report who gains and loses under multiple definitions.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tra.2020.04.009

## D09 · A Cost-Benefit Analysis of the Stockholm Congestion Charging System

- **作者 / 年份**：J. Eliasson (2009)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part A: Policy and Practice
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.tra.2008.11.014
- **影响力定位**：Highly influential observed-data evaluation of congestion charging.
- **研究问题**：Whether a real congestion-pricing scheme generates net social benefits.
- **治理启示**：Judge pricing with observed traffic, travel time, revenue, operating cost, safety and emissions rather than traffic reduction alone.
- **适用场景**：Use when designing pricing evaluation and benefit-cost evidence.
- **误用警告**：Transferability depends on geography, alternatives, charge design, implementation cost and public finance.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tra.2008.11.014

## D10 · The Stockholm Congestion-Charging Trial 2006: Overview of Effects

- **作者 / 年份**：J. Eliasson, L. Hultkrantz, L. Nerhagen, L. Smidfelt Rosqvist (2009)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Transportation Research Part A: Policy and Practice
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.tra.2008.09.007
- **影响力定位**：Landmark full-scale congestion-pricing field evidence.
- **研究问题**：Observed network and public-response effects of a time-differentiated cordon charge.
- **治理启示**：A policy trial can change both congestion and acceptance; visible travel-time reliability gains matter for legitimacy.
- **适用场景**：Use for cordon pricing, pilot design, public acceptance and before-after evaluation.
- **误用警告**：Do not assume the same acceptance trajectory where transit alternatives or governance conditions differ.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tra.2008.09.007

## D11 · The London Congestion Charge

- **作者 / 年份**：J. Leape (2006)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Journal of Economic Perspectives
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1257/jep.20.4.157
- **影响力定位**：Widely cited policy account in a leading economics journal.
- **研究问题**：How central London implemented congestion charging and what effects followed.
- **治理启示**：Implementation design, exemptions, enforcement and political feasibility are part of traffic governance, not afterthoughts.
- **适用场景**：Use for policy narratives, implementation constraints and charge-zone governance.
- **误用警告**：A city-center charging case should not be copied mechanically to polycentric cities.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.20.4.157

## D12 · An Integrated Model of Downtown Parking and Traffic Congestion

- **作者 / 年份**：R. Arnott, E. Inci (2006)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Journal of Urban Economics
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.jue.2006.04.004
- **影响力定位**：Influential parking-congestion economics paper.
- **研究问题**：How underpriced curb parking creates cruising traffic and congestion.
- **治理启示**：Parking price and occupancy are traffic-control variables; curb search traffic should be included in congestion diagnosis.
- **适用场景**：Use for CBD parking pricing, curb management and search-traffic mitigation.
- **误用警告**：Simplified downtown assumptions require local occupancy, turnover and search behavior data.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jue.2006.04.004

## D13 · Downtown Parking in Auto City

- **作者 / 年份**：R. Arnott, J. Rowse (2009)
- **期刊 / 会议**：Regional Science and Urban Economics
- **DOI / 来源**：10.1016/j.regsciurbeco.2008.08.001
- **影响力定位**：Influential extension of parking-congestion theory.
- **研究问题**：Joint effects of curb parking, garage parking, cruising and downtown traffic.
- **治理启示**：Treat curb and off-street parking as a coupled market; changing one price can shift search and occupancy elsewhere.
- **适用场景**：Use for district parking portfolios rather than isolated curb-price changes.
- **误用警告**：Stylized equilibrium results need empirical validation before operational target setting.
- **原始链接**：https://doi.org/10.1016/j.regsciurbeco.2008.08.001
